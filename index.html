About Us

Learn more about our mission and the story behind our platform

Founded in 2018, Learn SEO For Non Tech emerged from a simple observation: most SEO training programs were overly technical and intimidating for small business owners and marketing professionals without coding experience. Our founder, Sarah Chen, spent years helping non-technical entrepreneurs navigate the complex world of search engine optimization. Frustrated by the lack of accessible resources, she partnered with industry experts to create a comprehensive yet beginner-friendly learning platform. Today, we've trained over 50,000 students from 120+ countries, helping them achieve measurable improvements in their online visibility and business growth.

Our mission is to democratize SEO knowledge and empower every business owner, marketer, and content creator to master search engine optimization without technical barriers. We believe that SEO shouldn't be complicated, expensive, or exclusive to large corporations. We're committed to providing high-quality, practical education that delivers real results. Our core values – accessibility, clarity, and practical application – guide every course we develop. We envision a world where anyone with determination can build a thriving online presence through effective SEO strategies, regardless of their technical background or budget.
